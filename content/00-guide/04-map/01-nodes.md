## Nodes and tiers

A *node* is one definition, theorem, example or open question. Each node has a tag, such as SG0000000A, a statement in English, the Lean names that it needs, the papers that it comes from, and the nodes that it uses. The map is the set of nodes with the relation "uses". The relation has no cycle.

The nodes are in tiers. A tier is a part of the theory, and it has its main place in one or more volumes of this handbook.

| Tier | Content | Volume |
|---|---|---|
| S | the statements of the problem | 0 |
| T0 | books and surveys | 0 |
| T1 | commutative algebra and schemes | 1 |
| T2 | valuations | 3 |
| T3 | combinatorics: Newton polyhedra, fans, games | 7 and 9 |
| T4 | the invariants of characteristic $p$ | 7 |
| T5 | curves, surfaces and threefolds | 1, 4 and 6 |
| T6 | local uniformization and patching | 3 and 5 |
| T7 | alterations | 8 |
| T8 | characteristic 0 and the modern methods | 2 |

The site singularity.tamnd.com has a page for each node, with its status. The status changes when the Lean build changes. The handbook gives the status at the date of the edition only.
