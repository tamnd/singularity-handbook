## The routes

A *route* is a plan of attack on the open case. The project follows nine routes. Each route has an idea, the known results, a key obstruction and the first steps. Volume 9 reports on each route at each milestone, also when the result is negative.

| Route | Idea |
|---|---|
| R1. Characteristic polyhedra | Use Hironaka's characteristic polyhedron as the invariant, as Cossart and Piltant do in dimension 3. |
| R2. Local uniformization and patching | Prove local uniformization in dimension 4 and patch the local solutions, as Zariski did in characteristic 0. |
| R3. Rees algebras, elimination and idealistic filtrations | Find an invariant from the differential structure of the ideal that replaces maximal contact. |
| R4. Weighted and cobordant blowups | Find a weighted invariant in characteristic $p$ that goes down under a weighted blowup. |
| R5. The purely inseparable core | Solve the hypersurfaces $z^p + g = 0$ first, with the residual order and its corrections. |
| R6. Alterations and descent | Start from an alteration of degree a power of $p$ and descend to a resolution. |
| R7. Toric, arc and non-archimedean methods | Use the combinatorial and the non-archimedean structure of the singularity. |
| R8. Mixed characteristic | Use the methods of mixed characteristic, where dimension 3 is known. |
| R9. Special classes in every dimension | Solve classes such as binomial and toric varieties in every dimension, as tests of the methods. |

Three nodes of the map mark the first open targets of the routes: the purely inseparable hypersurfaces (route R5), local uniformization in dimension 4 (route R2), and a weighted invariant in characteristic $p$ (route R4).
