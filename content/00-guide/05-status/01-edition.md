## This edition

The second edition of Volume 0 and the first edition of Volume 9 went with the milestone S1 of the project. At S1 the project had these results in Lean, in addition to the statements and the fidelity checks of S0.

1. Resolution in dimension 0, in each characteristic (Theorem 2.3.1).
2. Resolution of integral varieties in characteristic 0, from the Lean proof of Hironaka's Main Theorem I in resolutionorg/hironaka (Theorem 2.3.2).
3. Resolution in characteristic 0 for all varieties, from the named hypothesis that reduces the problem to integral varieties (now Theorem 2.3.3, and Theorem 2.3.4). At S1 the Lean proof of the hypothesis was planned, so the map gave this result the status "proved-mod". Release 0.2.5 of the Lean package proves the reduction (Theorem 2.3.3), and the status is now "proved".
4. The bridge to google-deepmind/formal-conjectures (Proposition 2.5.4), and Hironaka's theorem in the form of formal-conjectures, with no hypothesis (Corollary 2.5.5).

Volume 9, Chapter 1 gives the complete proofs of these results, with examples, and it explains how each Lean proof follows the argument.

The open case has this status.
