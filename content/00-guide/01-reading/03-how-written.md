## How the handbook is written

The project uses AI tools to write drafts of the Lean code and of this handbook. A person sets the plan, reads the drafts and decides what is published. Lean checks the proofs, so a Lean proof does not depend on who wrote it. A text does depend on who wrote it. For this reason a chapter is "reviewed" only after a mathematician has read it and signed it, and the name of that person is in the front matter of each section.

The handbook uses plain technical English. The sentences are short, and each term has one meaning. A term is defined before it is used, or the text gives the place of its definition.
