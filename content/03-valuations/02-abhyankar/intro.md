# The Abhyankar inequality

This chapter proves the Abhyankar inequality. For a valuation $v$ of a function field $K/k$ it says
$$\operatorname{rr} \Gamma_v + \operatorname{trdeg}(Kv/k) \le \operatorname{trdeg}(K/k).$$
Section 2.1 proves a relative form for each extension of valued fields, with one lemma on independent elements. Section 2.2 studies the case of equality and defines Abhyankar valuations. Section 2.3 proves the local form of Abhyankar, for a valuation that dominates a Noetherian local domain. Section 2.4 gives examples of equality and of strict inequality, and it classifies the valuations of the plane.
