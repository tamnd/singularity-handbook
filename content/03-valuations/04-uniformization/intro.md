# Valuations and local uniformization

This chapter connects the theory of the earlier chapters to resolution of singularities. Section 4.1 defines local uniformization and proves the first step of the method of Zariski: if each valuation has local uniformization, then finitely many models are enough. It also states the known cases in low dimension. Section 4.2 states the two results on local uniformization that hold in all dimensions, the theorem of Knaf and Kuhlmann for Abhyankar valuations and the theorem of Temkin with a purely inseparable extension. It explains why defect is the obstruction in the other cases.

We prove no theorem of local uniformization in this volume. The proofs are long, and they belong to Volume 5. The open cases are in Volume 9, Chapter 2.
