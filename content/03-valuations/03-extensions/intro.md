# Extensions of valuations

This chapter studies how a valuation extends to a finite extension of fields. Section 3.1 gives the fundamental inequality between the degree, the ramification indices and the residue degrees. Section 3.2 defines the defect, which measures the failure of equality, and it computes the standard example of an Artin-Schreier extension with defect in characteristic $p$. Section 3.3 gives the key polynomials of MacLane. They describe the extensions of a valuation to a simple extension $K(y)$ step by step.
