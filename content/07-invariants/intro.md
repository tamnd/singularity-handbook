# Introduction

In characteristic $0$, resolution is proved by induction on the dimension. At a point of maximal order of a hypersurface, a hypersurface of maximal contact exists, the problem descends to an ideal on it, and a string of orders in descending dimensions is a resolution invariant that drops under each blowup. In characteristic $p > 0$ this method fails at several places. Hypersurfaces of maximal contact need not exist, and the second component of the invariant, the residual order, can increase under a blowup.

This volume studies the invariants that the proposed proofs in positive characteristic use. Chapter 1 defines the order of a power series, the Newton polyhedron and the residual order of a purely inseparable equation $z^{p^e} + F(x_1, \dots, x_n)$. It gives Moh's bound for the increase of the residual order under one blowup, a short computation that shows the increase, and the cycle of Hauser and Perlega, which disproves Moh's claim that the residual order is stable.

The reader we have in mind knows Volume 1, in particular the order of an element (Section 3.1) and the Frobenius (Chapter 6). Each result that has a Lean proof gives the name of its declaration in tamnd/singularity-lean.
