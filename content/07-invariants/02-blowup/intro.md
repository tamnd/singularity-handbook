# The order under a point blowup

A resolution invariant must not go up under the blowups that the process makes. For the first component, the order, this is a theorem in every characteristic. Section 1.3 used it without proof. In this chapter we prove it for a point blowup of affine space, and we find the points over the center where the order stays the same. These points lie in the projective space of a linear subspace, the directrix of the initial form. In characteristic $p$, the directrix can be larger than in characteristic $0$, and the example of Section 2.2 shows this.

Section 2.1 describes the point blowup in charts and the weak transform. Section 2.2 proves that the order does not go up, and it gives the directrix.

Throughout the chapter, $K$ is a field, $x = (x_1, \dots, x_n)$ and $K[x]$ is the polynomial ring. For a polynomial $f$ and $a \in K^n$, $\operatorname{ord}_a f$ is the order of $f$ at $a$, as in Section 1.1, and $\operatorname{ord} f = \operatorname{ord}_0 f$. For $f \ne 0$ we write $f = \sum_{d \ge 0} f_d$, where $f_d$ is homogeneous of degree $d$.
