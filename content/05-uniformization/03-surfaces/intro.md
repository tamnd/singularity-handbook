# Surfaces in characteristic p

Abhyankar proved local uniformization for surfaces over an algebraically closed field of characteristic $p > 0$ [Abh56s]. This is Theorem 4.1.4 of Volume 3. In characteristic $p$ the method of Section 1.3 can fail for a new reason: the extension $K/K_0$ of a function field over a field of rational functions can have a defect. Then there is no étale generator, and local uniformization does not lift in the simple way of Theorem 1.3.4.

Section 3.1 states the theorem of Abhyankar. It shows that the theorem of Lipman on the resolution of excellent surfaces (Volume 4, Theorem 2.2.1) gives local uniformization of surfaces over each field, and it proves a converse of Lemma 1.3.3: an étale generator exists exactly when $e = 1$, the residue extension is separable, and $d = 1$. Section 3.2 shows by examples that the defect depends on the choice of the subfield $K_0$, and it connects the defect with the examples of Volumes 3, 4 and 9.

We do not prove the theorem of Abhyankar in general. The proofs in this chapter are complete, except that Proposition 3.1.2 uses the theorem of Lipman, which Volume 4 proves.
