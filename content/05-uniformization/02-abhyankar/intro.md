# Abhyankar valuations

An Abhyankar valuation is a valuation for which the Abhyankar inequality is an equality (Volume 3, Definition 2.2.3). Its value group is $\mathbb{Z}^r$ and its residue field is finitely generated (Volume 3, Corollary 2.2.4). These valuations are the ones closest to monomial valuations, and local uniformization for them holds in each characteristic.

Section 2.1 treats monomial valuations of a field of rational functions. The proof is combinatorial. We show that finitely many positive elements of an ordered group $\mathbb{Z}^r$ lie in the cone of a basis of positive elements. A change of variables by the corresponding monomials then puts the given finite set into a polynomial ring whose center is the origin. Section 2.2 proves the theorem of Knaf-Kuhlmann [KK05] when the residue field is separable over the ground field. The valuation is monomial on a suitable subfield $K_0$, and the extension $K/K_0$ has an étale generator. Then the lifting theorem of Section 1.3 gives local uniformization. The existence of the étale generator rests on the generalized stability theorem of Kuhlmann [Kuh10], which we cite.

The Lean library defines Abhyankar valuations (`Singularity.IsAbhyankarValuation`) and states their local uniformization (`Singularity.localUniformization_abhyankar`). It proves the Abhyankar inequality (`Singularity.abhyankar_inequality`). It proves the lattice lemma (Section 2.1, Lemma 2.1.4), the monomial case for value groups of each rank (Theorem 2.1.5), and the fact that a monomial valuation is an Abhyankar valuation (part 3 of Lemma 2.1.2). The other results of this chapter are not in the library.
