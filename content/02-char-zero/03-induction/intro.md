# The induction on the dimension

This chapter proves principalization in characteristic 0. By Theorem 2.3.3, a blowup sequence of order $m$ for an ideal $I$ of maximal order $m$ takes place inside a hypersurface of maximal contact $H$. We find an ideal on $H$ whose blowup sequences are exactly those of $I$, and we reduce the problem on $X$ to a problem on $H$, of dimension one less.

Section 3.1 proves the restriction theorem: for any smooth hypersurface $S$, the points of order at least $m$ of the transforms of $I$ on the transforms of $S$ are described by the restrictions of the derivative ideals $D^j(I)$ to $S$, with the marks $m - j$. Section 3.2 packs these restrictions into one ideal, the coefficient ideal, and proves that order reduction on $H$ gives order reduction on $X$. It also explains why the choice of $H$ does not matter. Section 3.3 assembles the induction: order reduction for marked ideals in dimension $n - 1$ gives order reduction for ideals in dimension $n$, and this gives order reduction for marked ideals in dimension $n$. The case of the mark 1 is principalization. Section 3.3 ends with a complete run of the algorithm on the ideal $(x^2, y^3)$, with the invariant $s$ and the centers at each step.

We follow [Kol07, Chapter 3]. Kollár works with the ideals $W_s(I)$ and with D-balanced ideals; we use the classical coefficient ideal of Villamayor [Vil89] and Encinas-Hauser [EH02], for which the proofs of the going-up and going-down theorems are short. For the uniqueness of the construction and for the passage from local to global, we state Kollár's results and sketch their proofs.
