# The open case

This chapter is about the problem that the project has not solved. We state the problem again, we give the cases that are known, and we explain why the proofs of characteristic 0 do not extend to fourfolds in characteristic $p$. Then we describe the main methods that mathematicians use on the problem, and we say what each method still needs.

No proof of $\mathrm{Res}(p, 4)$ is known. This chapter does not give one, and the project does not claim one. The chapter has some proofs, but they are proofs of known results and of small facts about examples.

Section 2.1 gives the known cases in dimension at most 3. Section 2.2 explains the failures of the methods of characteristic 0, each with an example. Section 2.3 describes the routes to dimension 4 and the weaker results that are known in all dimensions. Section 2.4 gives the special classes of varieties that have a resolution in all dimensions, and it says what the project does next.

The reader needs Volume 7, Chapter 1, for the residual order and the examples of Hauser and Perlega. For valuations we recall the definitions in Section 2.3. Volume 3 gives the theory with proofs: centers and the Zariski-Riemann space in Chapter 1, Abhyankar valuations in Chapter 2, defect in Section 3.2 and local uniformization in Chapter 4.
