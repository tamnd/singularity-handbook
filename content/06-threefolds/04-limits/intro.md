# Why the method stops at dimension three

The method of Cossart and Piltant is written for a regular local ring of dimension $n = 3$, that is, for threefolds. This chapter explains which steps use $n = 3$, and what is known for $n = 4$. Section 4.2 gives the questions that Volume 9 studies. Section 4.3 reports a computation of the invariants of Chapter 3 on random examples for $n = 3$ and $n = 4$.
