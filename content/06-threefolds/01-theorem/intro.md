# The theorem and its history

This chapter states the resolution of threefolds in characteristic $p > 0$ and tells how it was proved. Section 1.1 gives the statements of Abhyankar, Cutkosky and Cossart-Piltant. Section 1.2 gives the plan of the proof of Abhyankar and Cutkosky, and it proves the one step of the plan that needs $p > 5$: at a point of multiplicity $m < p$ there is a hypersurface of maximal contact.
