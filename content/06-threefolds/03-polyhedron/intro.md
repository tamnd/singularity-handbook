# The characteristic polyhedron and the invariants

This chapter defines the polyhedron of Hironaka for a monic polynomial over a regular local ring, and the invariants that Cossart and Piltant build on it. Section 3.1 defines the polyhedron, proves the vertex preparation step, and shows that the Tschirnhausen transformation gives a polyhedron with no solvable vertex when the degree is a unit, so that it is minimal by the theorem of Hironaka. It also finds the solvable vertices of the purely inseparable equations $X^p + f$. Section 3.2 defines the invariants $\delta$, $d_j$, $\epsilon$ and $\omega$ for the purely inseparable equations, and proves that cleaning gives the minimal polyhedron in this case. It proves the first step of Theorem 3.2.7 when $\epsilon = 0$, states the Projection Theorem, and shows how the theorems of Cossart and Piltant combine in an induction on $\iota$. Section 3.3 computes examples by hand: a point blowup at which $\epsilon$ increases, the example of Cossart and Piltant on $\omega$, a characteristic polyhedron over $\mathbb{F}_3$, and a point blowup at which $\iota$ drops.
