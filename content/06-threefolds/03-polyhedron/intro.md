# The characteristic polyhedron and the invariants

This chapter defines the polyhedron of Hironaka for a monic polynomial over a regular local ring, and the invariants that Cossart and Piltant build on it. Section 3.1 defines the polyhedron, proves the vertex preparation step, and proves that the Tschirnhausen transformation gives the minimal polyhedron when the degree is a unit. Section 3.2 defines the invariants $\delta$, $d_j$, $\epsilon$ and $\omega$ for the purely inseparable equations, and proves that cleaning gives the minimal polyhedron in this case. Section 3.3 computes examples by hand.
