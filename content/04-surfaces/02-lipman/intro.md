# The theorem of Lipman

This chapter states the theorem of Lipman: each reduced excellent Noetherian scheme of dimension at most 2 has a resolution, and one obtains it if one normalizes and blows up the singular points, in turn, until the scheme is regular. Section 2.1 defines rational singularities, proves the formal parts of the reduction to the rational case, and shows that rationality can be checked at the local rings. Section 2.2 states the theorem, proves the parts that are formal, and says which steps of the proof we only cite. Section 2.3 computes examples in characteristic $p$ in charts. Two of them compute $H^1$ of a resolution: it is a line for a singularity whose exceptional curve has genus 1, and it is 0 for the singularity $xy = z^2$.
