# The theorem of Lipman

This chapter states the theorem of Lipman: each reduced excellent Noetherian scheme of dimension at most 2 has a resolution, and one obtains it if one normalizes and blows up the singular points, in turn, until the scheme is regular. Section 2.1 defines rational singularities and proves the formal parts of the reduction to the rational case. Section 2.2 states the theorem, proves the parts that are formal, and says which steps of the proof we only cite. Section 2.3 computes examples in characteristic $p$ in charts.
