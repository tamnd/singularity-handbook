# Blowups and normal surfaces

This chapter gives the two operations of the resolution of surfaces: the blowup of a point and the normalization. Section 1.1 recalls the blowup along a closed subscheme and its universal property, and it computes the blowup of a regular surface at a closed point. The exceptional curve is a projective line with self-intersection $-1$. Section 1.2 studies normal surfaces. The normalization of an excellent surface is finite, and a normal excellent surface has only finitely many singular points. These two facts make the process of Chapter 2 well defined.
