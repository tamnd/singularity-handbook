# Embedded resolution

The process of Chapter 2 changes the surface, but it does not keep an embedding of the surface in a regular scheme. This chapter states the embedded form of the resolution of surfaces, the theorem of Cossart, Jannsen and Saito, and it gives the history of the resolution of surfaces. Two examples compute embedded resolutions in charts. The second one, the Whitney umbrella, needs a center of dimension 1: no sequence of point blowups resolves it in its embedding. For curves on a smooth surface over an algebraically closed field the chapter proves the embedded resolution by blowups of points, with the decrease of the multiplicity and of the delta invariant, and it works out the curve $(y^2 - x^3)(y^2 - x^5)$.
