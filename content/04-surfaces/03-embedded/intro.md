# Embedded resolution

The process of Chapter 2 changes the surface, but it does not keep an embedding of the surface in a regular scheme. This chapter states the embedded form of the resolution of surfaces, the theorem of Cossart, Jannsen and Saito, and it gives the history of the resolution of surfaces. Two examples compute embedded resolutions in charts. The second one, the Whitney umbrella, needs a center of dimension 1: no sequence of point blowups resolves it in its embedding.
