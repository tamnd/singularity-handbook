## The start

A regular scheme is its own resolution.

**Theorem (identity).** If $X$ is regular, the identity of $X$ is a resolution of $X$.

Node: SG0000000A · Lean: `Singularity.isResolution_id`

*Proof.* The identity is proper and birational, and $X$ is regular. $\square$

**Definition.** A scheme is *tame* when it is regular.
